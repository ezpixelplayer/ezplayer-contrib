⏱️ Live countdown settings

A big countdown on a matrix of the show to a moment: a date and time, every day at a time, or a few minutes after you press Start below (for the couple's entrance or the cake). From a few minutes before, the matrix shows the time left (the last ten seconds as one huge number); at zero, fireworks, a message and a fanfare. In between the show plays. The visitors' page counts down with the lights. Pick the matrix, then Save.
