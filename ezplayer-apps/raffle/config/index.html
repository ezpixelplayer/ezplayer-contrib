🎟️ Raffle settings

A prize draw on a matrix of the show. The entries are your list of names, ticket numbers, visitors who join from their phone with their name, or your list and the visitors. Only you start a draw: with the Draw now button below or a show button (the input "draw"). The names spin fast across the matrix, slow down and stop on the winner, shown big with sparkles. Pick the matrix and the entries, then Save.
