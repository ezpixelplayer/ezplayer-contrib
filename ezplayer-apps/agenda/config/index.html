🗓️ Agenda settings

Give every app its times on one page. The times are kept by the player itself (the same as the Apps card), so they work even when Agenda is off. Dates ("only from 1 Dec to 6 Jan") are Agenda's own: they only work while Agenda runs, so keep Agenda itself on Always.

Quick presets

A preset fills in the default times (or the choices below); nothing changes until you Save.

Default times

Every app that "follows the default" is on during these times. No times at all means always.

Apps

A window that goes past midnight (18:00 to 02:00) is kept as two: until 24:00, and from 00:00 the next day.

When each app is on, with the times on this page (also the ones not saved yet). Dates are not shown here.
